How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

  1. Your dealer fits it

    A small tracker is fitted out of sight on the scooter or powered wheelchair. It takes about the length of a normal service.

  2. We watch over it

    Day and night, MobilityGuard checks the battery, how it's being charged, and whether the scooter has tipped or taken a knock.

  3. The right people get a text

    The rider hears first. If something isn't sorted, the family are told too, in plain English.

What we look out for

The things that leave people stranded.

Most scooter call-outs come down to a flat or worn-out battery. That's where MobilityGuard does its most careful work.

Battery health, before it fails

We track how the batteries hold their charge over weeks and months, so a failing battery is spotted early and swapped before it lets someone down halfway to the shops.

Example battery health82 / 100 · Good

Low battery and charging reminders

A text when it's running low, an evening nudge to plug in, and a weekly top-up reminder. If a reminder is missed, a carer is told.

Tip-over or a hard knock

If the scooter goes over or takes a big jolt, everyone on the list is texted at once.

Where is it?

Carers can ask for the last known location, but only if the rider has said yes. It's switched off until they do.

Help button In testing

Who gets told

Up to five people, in the right order.

Everyday reminders go to the rider first. Carers only hear about it if it isn't sorted. Anything urgent goes to everyone straight away.

Your privacy

Their location stays private.

  • Location history is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Dealers never see where the rider is. Only the MobilityGuard team can.
  • Carers can only ask for a location if the rider has agreed to it.
  • Riders can opt out of texts at any time by sending STOP.
