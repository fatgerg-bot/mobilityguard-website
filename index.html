What it covers

The four things families worry about.

MobilityGuard does a lot, so here it is the way you'd think about it: the questions that go through your head when someone you love heads out on their own.

Will he get home?

Battery care that stops breakdowns before they happen.

  • "Time to head home" texts to the rider when the battery is getting low for the distance home.
  • Reminders at 25% and 10%, an evening nudge to plug in, and a carer told if it's missed.
  • Battery health checked every month, so a worn battery is swapped before it lets them down.
  • A care score for charging habits, plus warnings about a faulty charger or a very cold battery.
What if she tips over?

Help is on its way in seconds.

  • Crash, tip-over and fall alerts go to up to three carers at once, with the location.
  • An optional SOS button the rider can press, which works on its own so nothing can hold it up.
  • Carers can tap "I'm dealing with it" so everyone else knows it's in hand.
  • Key details for 999 can be sent to carers with the alert, if the rider agrees.
Where has Mum got to?

Messages when they arrive and leave the places that matter.

  • Save places like home, the surgery or a friend's house. You'll hear when they get there and when they leave.
  • "Not home yet" if the scooter's still out after the time you choose.
  • A safe area alert if the scooter goes further than usual, all day or just at night.
  • Only ever shared if the rider says yes.
What if someone takes it?

Security that knows the difference between a ride and a theft.

  • Tow alert if the scooter is moved while it's switched off.
  • Tamper alert if the battery or the unit is disconnected.
  • The location is included on every urgent alert, ready to pass to the police.
Pre-emptive battery care

Look after the battery, and it looks after them.

Most scooter breakdowns come down to a worn or badly treated battery. MobilityGuard watches it every day, so problems are fixed before anyone is left stranded.

  • Active monitoringCharge, range and charging, checked day and night. You and your dealer can see it any time.
  • Health checks every monthTested while parked, while riding, and by comparing the two batteries. That tells a worn battery apart from a soft tyre or a dragging brake.
  • Caught earlyA battery left flat, a charger that isn't working, or charging habits that shorten its life are picked up straight away, with a gentle nudge to the rider and family.
  • Fewer breakdowns
  • Batteries last longer
  • No paying for a battery you didn't need
