What it covers

The four things families worry about.

Will he get home?

Battery care that stops breakdowns before they happen.

  • "Time to head home" texts to the rider when the battery is getting low for the distance home.
  • Reminders at 25% and 10%, an evening nudge to plug in, and a carer told if it's missed.
  • Battery health checked every month, so a worn battery is swapped before it lets them down.
  • A care score for charging habits, plus warnings about a faulty charger or a very cold battery.
What if she tips over?

Carers are alerted within seconds.

  • Crash, tip-over and fall alerts go to up to three carers at once, with the location.
  • An optional SOS button the rider can press, which works on its own so nothing can hold it up.
  • Carers can tap "I'm dealing with it" so everyone else knows it's in hand.
  • Key details for 999 can be sent to carers with the alert, if the rider agrees.
Where has Mum got to?

Messages when they arrive and leave the places that matter.

  • Save places like home, the surgery or a friend's house. You'll hear when they get there and when they leave.
  • "Not home yet" if the scooter's still out after the time you choose.
  • A safe area alert if the scooter goes further than usual, all day or just at night.
  • Only ever shared if the rider says yes.
What if someone takes it?

Security that knows the difference between a ride and a theft.

  • Tow alert if the scooter is moved while it's switched off.
  • Tamper alert if the battery or the unit is disconnected.
  • The location is included on every urgent alert, ready to pass to the police.
Battery care

Spot battery trouble before it strands anyone.

Most scooter breakdowns come down to a worn or badly treated battery. MobilityGuard watches it every day and nudges at the right moment, so the battery lasts longer and is replaced before anyone is left stranded.

  • Active monitoringCharge, range and charging, checked day and night. You and your dealer can see it any time.
  • Health checks every monthTested while parked, while riding, and by comparing the two batteries, so a worn battery is spotted early.
  • Caught earlyA battery left flat, a charger that isn't working or charging habits that shorten its life are picked up straight away, with a gentle nudge to the rider and family. That matters from day one: even a brand-new battery left flat can sulphate and be ruined within months.
  • Fewer breakdowns
  • Batteries replaced less often

The unit

Small enough to hide. Clever enough to notice.

Everything starts with one slim box, a little smaller than a mobile phone. Your dealer fits it inside the scooter, where nobody sees it, and it quietly keeps watch from then on.

  1. Fitted out of sightYour dealer tucks it inside the scooter and wires it to the scooter's own battery. The rider has nothing to wear.
  2. It noticesA built-in motion sensor feels a tip-over or a hard knock, and it knows when the key is turned on.
  3. It knows whereSatellite positioning (GPS) works out where the scooter is, and a built-in 4G connection sends that, and the battery readings, to us.
  4. We do the thinkingOur system checks everything, decides what matters, and sends one clear text or app message to the right person.

Questions

Questions people ask us

How long do mobility scooter batteries last?

Most mobility scooter batteries last around 18 months to 2 years. How they're charged makes a big difference: a battery that's often left flat, or charged on a faulty charger, can wear out in a few months. MobilityGuard checks the battery every day and sends a gentle reminder when it needs charging, so it lasts as long as it should.

Why is my mobility scooter battery not charging?

The usual causes are a faulty charger, a loose plug or lead, a tripped reset switch or fuse, or a battery that has been left flat for too long. Your dealer can check it for you. With MobilityGuard fitted, your dealer can see the charging history and can often tell whether it's the charger or the battery before they visit.

What if my scooter has a lithium battery?

Most scooters today use AGM or gel batteries, and that's what MobilityGuard is set up for now. Lithium batteries are becoming more common, and we're developing support for them.

Lithium batteries have one big advantage: a built-in battery management system (BMS) that protects them from overcharging, running completely flat and charging when it's too cold. But a BMS can't stop everything. Cells drift out of balance over time, packs wear out with age and heat, and a battery left flat for weeks may lock itself out and refuse to charge. When the BMS steps in to protect the battery, the scooter may simply stop, even in the middle of a trip.

So the idea behind MobilityGuard stays the same, whatever the battery is made of: spot the warning signs early and look after the battery before it lets anyone down. With lithium that matters even more. A pair of lead-acid batteries usually costs £50 to £350 to replace, while a lithium battery can cost £350 to £1,650.

Is there a monthly fee?

Yes, a small monthly fee covers the mobile connection, the text messages and the app. We'll confirm the price at launch. It will cost less each month than most GPS personal alarms, which typically charge £25 to £32 a month plus a set-up fee, and you'll be able to cancel at any time.

Does the rider need a smartphone?

No. Everything works by text message on any mobile phone, even an old button phone. The app is there for anyone who'd like to see more: there's an Android app, and a web app for iPhone.

What if there's no mobile signal?

The unit isn't tied to one network. It uses whichever mobile network is available where the scooter is, so being out of signal is much less likely. If it ever is, MobilityGuard carries on watching the battery and catches up as soon as it's back in signal.

Can I see where they are all the time?

Only if the rider says yes. Sharing their location with carers stays switched off until the rider agrees. The one exception is a crash, fall or SOS alert, which always includes the scooter’s location so carers can help. Location history is deleted automatically after 30 days. Dealers never see where the rider is.

What happens if the scooter is stolen?

If the scooter moves when it shouldn't, MobilityGuard sends an alert straight away with where it is. Call the police and give them the location. Please don't try to get the scooter back yourself.
