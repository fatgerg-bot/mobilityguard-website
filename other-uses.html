Got battery-powered kit left out on its own?

The same tracker and battery checks work on other kit that runs from a 12 V or 24 V battery. You see where each one is, how its battery is doing, and get told when something's wrong.

Road works temporary traffic lights

Portable lights sit on site for days or weeks, running from batteries. A flat battery means lights out and a crew sent back in a hurry, and a stolen set is gone before anyone notices.

  • Battery level from the officeSee every set's battery, and get a warning when one is running low, so you swap it before the lights go dark.
  • Every set on one mapKnow which site each set is on, and find it fast at the end of a job.
  • Moved or takenAn alert if a set is lifted or driven away when it should be standing still.
  • Battery unpluggedAn alert if the battery is disconnected or taken from the box.
  • Which batteries to replaceA history for each battery shows which ones hold their charge and which ones are wearing out.

Stairlifts

Most run on 24 V batteries that charge at the top or bottom of the stairs. Get warned when the batteries stop charging or start wearing out, before the lift stops halfway.

Site lighting and CCTV towers

Battery and solar towers on building sites and car parks. See the battery level and get told if a tower is moved.

Battery plant and hire kit

Scissor lifts, floor scrubbers and other battery machines out on hire. Know where each one is and whether it's been left flat.

Hire boats and narrowboats

Keep an eye on leisure batteries between hires, and see where each boat is moored.

Event and site power packs

Portable battery packs and barriers out at events. Check charge before the day and get an alert if one walks off.

Speed signs and message boards

Battery signs on trailers at road works and in villages. See the battery level and get an alert if one is hitched up and taken.

Electric fence energisers

Battery boxes left out in fields. Get warned before the battery dies and the fence goes off, and if a box is taken.

Farm quad bikes

Know where each bike is and get an alert if one is moved at night. Crash and fall alerts can also tell someone if a rider working alone comes off.

Ride-on mowers and grounds kit

For councils, schools and grounds teams. An alert if a mower leaves the depot out of hours, and a check on its battery.
